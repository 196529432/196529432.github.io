隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

更新日期 / Last updated: 2026-10-16

1. 应用与使用者

WLRobot-Rclone 是账户持有人个人使用的 rclone 桌面连接配置，目的是在个人 Windows 电脑和本人 Google Drive 之间传输 WLRobot 项目的技术验收文件。它不对公众提供账户或文件处理服务。

2. 访问的数据与权限

应用通过 Google Drive API，在用户授予的权限范围内读取 Drive 中的文件名称、元数据和内容，并可执行上传、下载、创建、更新、移动或删除文件等由账户持有人发起的操作。目前使用 Google Drive 完整文件访问权限；该权限不表示所有文件都会被自动访问。

3. 数据使用、存储与共享

读取和传输的数据仅用于个人文件管理及软件验收证据交接。rclone 在本人的电脑上执行，OAuth 凭据保存在本地 rclone 配置中。文件可以存在本人 Google Drive 和本地电脑中。本 OAuth 应用本身不运行用于收集文件的独立公共服务器，不出售 Google 用户数据，也不将其用于广告。

账户持有人可以独立授权其他应用（例如 ChatGPT 的 Google Drive 连接器）读取指定验收文件；那是另外的授权和处理流程，不由本 OAuth 客户端自动授予。

4. 数据保留与删除

Google Drive 文件由账户持有人自行决定保留或删除。本地临时文件可在确认云端上传和下载校验成功后清理。账户持有人可在 Google 账号的第三方应用连接管理中撤销本应用授权，并可删除本地 rclone OAuth 配置。

5. 安全

本应用不要求在此网站输入 Google 密码或 OAuth 凭据。账户持有人负责保护个人电脑上的 rclone 配置以及验收文件，避免公开分享敏感项目数据。

1. Purpose

WLRobot-Rclone is a personal configuration of the open-source rclone desktop client. Its owner uses it to transfer software review evidence between a personal Windows computer and the owner's Google Drive account. It does not offer services to the public.

2. Google user data and access

With the owner's Google OAuth authorization, rclone may read file names, metadata and content, and perform owner-directed upload, download, create, update, move, and delete operations within the granted full Google Drive access scope. Granting this scope does not cause automatic collection of every file.

3. Use, storage and sharing

Data is used for the owner's personal file operations and software review evidence handoff. rclone runs on the owner's own computer and stores OAuth credentials in its local configuration. Files may remain in the owner's Google Drive or on the local computer. This OAuth application does not operate a separate public data collection server, sell Google user data, or use it for advertising.

If the owner separately authorizes another service, such as a ChatGPT Google Drive connector, to access review files, that access is governed by a separate authorization and is not automatically granted by WLRobot-Rclone.

4. Retention, deletion and revocation

The owner decides which Drive files to keep or delete. Local temporary files can be removed after remote integrity verification. The owner can revoke Google access in their Google Account and delete the locally stored rclone OAuth configuration.

5. Security

This website does not request Google passwords or OAuth credentials. The owner is responsible for protecting local tokens and avoiding public sharing of sensitive project files.
