Personal desktop utility

WLRobot-Rclone

个人使用的 Google Drive 文件传输配置，用于保存和读取 WLRobot 软件开发过程中的验收 ZIP、校验文件和相关技术证据。

用途和运行方式

本工具使用开源 rclone 客户端及 Google Drive API，在账户持有人明确发起操作时，从个人电脑上传、下载或管理指定文件。该 OAuth 客户端仅供账户持有人个人使用，不提供其他用户注册服务。

Google Drive 访问权限由账户持有人在 Google 授权页面授予，可随时在 Google 账户中撤销。此网站仅作公开应用介绍；它不提供文件上传界面、不收集登录凭据，也不托管项目文件。

About this application

WLRobot-Rclone is a personal desktop Google Drive connection used by its owner to transfer and manage software review ZIP archives, checksum files, and related technical evidence. It uses the open-source rclone client and the Google Drive API. File operations are initiated by the account owner on their own computer.

This website is an informational landing page only. It does not host files, collect Google sign-in credentials, or offer public sign-up.
